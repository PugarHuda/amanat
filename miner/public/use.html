Plate VII · four ways in

Call it from your own agent.

Cheapest first. The reading is useful anywhere a decision waits on weather; the contract is only one thing to do with it.

  1. free

    Straight over HTTP

    POST /forecast with lat, lon and hours, or a question. No wallet, no key. Nothing is verified by validators on this route, and it costs nothing; those go together. The spec is at /openapi.json, the signing key at /.well-known/amanat.json.

  2. $0.01

    From an agent, over MCP

    The Telegraph MCP server discovers every registered miner, so Amanat is already a tool in Claude Desktop, Cursor, Continue, Goose and LangChain. It appears as tg_amanat_weather_risk_forecast.

  3. $0.01

    Through the Engine

    Ask in plain language and the protocol picks the miner. You may or may not get Amanat. That is the point, and it is how you find out whether we are any good.

  4. $1.00

    From a contract

    Escrow USDC, open a job against an intent, receive the answer through subnetMessage. The contract on this page is a working receiver you can copy.
